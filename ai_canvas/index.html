About this map

Built from a late-2026 digest of public surveys (Pew, Reuters Institute, Gallup), vendor usage reports (OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, Google) and education research (OECD and randomized trials). Numbers are rounded on purpose and age fast, so check the originals before quoting them. The analogies and questions are prompts for conversation, not findings.
