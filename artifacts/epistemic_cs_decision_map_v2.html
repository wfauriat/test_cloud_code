About this map

Version 2, 18 November 2026. An explorable map of one line of research. A talk (TK) seeds it; an article (FP) states the frame argument; an essay (WG) adds the computing stack and its learned top layer; a second essay (FD) re-reads both as a theory of checks. An external critique (CR) and a review of that critique and of this map's first version (RV) are folded in and tagged as such.

All written sources except CR come out of one collaboration between the author and Claude models. By the map's own Proposition 4, their agreement is weak evidence; where they depart from one another is the informative part. Numbers in the labs are illustrative.
